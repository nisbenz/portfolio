This site is my public notebook.

I'm a 4th-year CS undergraduate at USTHB (Algiers), specializing in AI, and
for the past couple of years I've been learning machine learning the slow
way: by building things from scratch. An autograd engine in pure C. A
transformer that translates English to Darija. An I-JEPA reimplementation. A
GPT-2 inference engine in C++ on top of ggml. Each one started as "I want to
understand how this actually works" and ended as a folder full of code,
benchmark numbers, and hard-won bugs.

The problem is that most of that understanding currently lives in my head
and in commit messages. That's a waste — both for me, because writing is how
I find out I don't understand something yet, and for whoever is one step
behind me on the same path.

So the plan for this blog:

- **Build logs** — how my projects actually work: TensorLib's autograd, SIMD
  matmul kernels, the unglamorous details papers skip.
- **Paper reimplementations** — what I learned from re-creating architectures
  by hand, and where the specs were ambiguous.
- **Student notes** — coursework, club projects at
  [Micro Club USTHB](https://github.com/MicroClub-USTHB), internships, and
  the occasional lesson that has nothing to do with code.

If any of that sounds interesting, the [RSS feed](/portfolio/rss.xml) is the
easiest way to follow along. And if you want to argue about any of it, my
email is in the footer — I genuinely enjoy being corrected.
